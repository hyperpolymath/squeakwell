= squeakwell Route Plan: proof pathways and the engineering route onto VeriSimDB
:toc: left
:toclevels: 3
:icons: font
:revdate: 2026-11-05

[.lead]
squeakwell recovers damaged datasets by scattering fragments across VeriSimDB's
eight modalities and letting them cross-check and reconstruct each other.
VeriSimDB is the required underlying database. This document answers four
questions: what is the fastest route down the proof pathways, what is the
fastest engineering route, what else has to be created across the estate, and
how an ultracode engineering session should be run. It replaces the aspirational
wording in `README.adoc` where the two disagree.

== 0. Provenance of this plan

What this plan rests on, so nothing here is taken for more than it is:

* Six reader agents read squeakwell, verisimdb (Rust crates and `formal/*.v`),
  vcl-ut, echo-types, tropical-types and the estate `standards`/mathesis policy,
  citing file and line. Four design agents each produced a full architecture.
  Three judges scored them under the elegance, dependability/privacy and
  speed/testability lenses. Fourteen adversarial refuters attacked the proposed
  theorem ladder; twelve claims were refuted *as packaged* and repaired, two
  survived. The repaired statements are the ones recorded here.
* Verified by running: `cargo check -p verisim-octad -p verisim-drift
  -p verisim-normalizer` succeeds (165 crate units, about 37 s warm cache);
  `vcltotal-parse` builds standalone (2 s); the `vclt-gate` binary certifies
  `DECLARE … WHERE … = $1 LIMIT 1` at level 6 and `NORMALISE 'e' FROM
  AUTHORITATIVE DOCUMENT` at level 4, rejects `EFFECTS { Write }` at L0, and
  spawns in about 3 ms; squeakwell builds and passes its 23 tests.
* Not verified by running: no Rocq/Coq, Agda, Lean, Idris2, Creusot, Isabelle or
  podman is installed in the planning environment. Every statement about a
  proof is from reading the source. Per `standards/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`
  section 5, every proof row for squeakwell is therefore OPEN until a prover
  actually runs.

== 1. The answer in one page

**Engineering route (fastest honest path to a working recovery).**
Build squeakwell as a *compiler from fragments to a certified VCL transition
program*, with a *local-first pure kernel* in front of it. Fragments are typed
and classified in-process (guarded fill, pure resolve, the lexicographic
measure), contradictions are held locally, and only agreed values are lowered
to a running `verisim-api` over its real routes. Every round is printed as a
canonical `.vcl` script, every statement is certified by the `vclt-gate` binary
before it is executed, every executed line becomes a provenance record on the
entity's hash chain, and the loop re-synthesises from the server's own drift
report until the program is empty. This is Architecture D with Architecture A's
kernel grafted in (section 4). Two of three judges chose D; the third chose B
and its grafts are the upstream asks in section 5. The MVP needs **no change in
verisimdb or vcl-ut**; everything on its critical path exists today.

**Proof pathway (fastest honest path to a first mechanised result).**
Do not try to prove the README's five invariants: one is false (drift is not
monotone under fill), two are vacuous as written (determinism as reflexivity,
termination as "or `max_iterations`"), one belongs to vcl-ut (the ratchet) and
one conflates a provable identity fact with an unprovable oracle claim. Prove
the repaired statements in section 3 in this order:

. **Rung 0 (today, Rust)**: property tests for the resolve-kernel laws, guarded
  fill, and the lexicographic measure, with the mandatory counter-model test
  that *finds* a case where conflicts rise after fill.
. **Rung 1 (hours, Rocq)**: `verisimdb/formal/Recovery.v`: guarded fill,
  normalise, identity preservation, the counter-model to drift monotonicity,
  and lexicographic progress with the hypotheses the refuters showed are
  needed (`conflict_sym`, winner-relative conflict count).
. **Rung 2 (one to two sessions, Agda)**: `echo-types/proofs/agda/EchoPartialSection.agda`,
  the join-semilattice of partial sections that `verisimdb/formal/CROSS-REPO-MAP.adoc`
  already flags as the one missing abstraction. The Rocq file then becomes an
  instantiation, as the owner's standing directive requires.
. **Rung 3 (hours each, Lean)**: the two small tropical-types lemmas that
  survived refutation: the free-monoid residue measure and the reachability
  grade with its monotonicity facts.
. **Rung 4 (after the kernel exists, Creusot)**: purity and permutation
  invariance of the Rust resolve kernel, and the export postcondition.
. **Rung 5 (after the wire format is stable, Idris2)**: the engineering
  contract for the program IR, journal and gate request/response.

**What else must be created**: section 5 lists it per repository. The largest
single item is the echo-types module; the most valuable upstream ask is a
`propose`/`apply` pair on verisimdb's normalizer that carries a
`RepairJustification`, because today the server picks the repair target itself.

**Decisions only the owner can take**: section 9. The default arm is labelled
in each.

=== 2.3 vcl-ut (the certifier)

* Every level L0–L10 is a predicate over a `Statement` (plus `OctadSchema`) or
  a `Transition`; none mentions a value or an octad. "Data that passes level N"
  is a category error. The honest claim is: *the reintroduction statement for
  entity e is gate-admissible at level k, and e's populated fields are
  schema-bound.*
* From VCL text only L0–L6 are reachable (issue #25, fail-closed). Transitions
  have a hard ceiling of 4 (`Transition.idr` 183-185) and do not check modality
  presence: `NORMALISE e FROM AUTHORITATIVE TENSOR` on a tensor-less entity is
  admissible. Exit 0 is not "repair justified".
* The `vclt-gate` binary is the frozen v1 seam; verisimdb's Elixir executor
  already calls it. Linking `vcltotal-parse` as a crate bypasses the trusted
  seam (its Rust decider carries no proofs). Use the binary; use the crate only
  as a test oracle.
* `Epistemic.idr` line 149 discards the atom in `satisfies (PAtom _)`, and
  `PBelieves` evaluates identically to `PKnows`; no module imports the Kripke
  semantics. Any L10 story built on it today is vacuous.

=== 2.4 echo-types and tropical-types (the owner's types)

* echo-types: `Echo f y := Σ x. f x ≡ y` and the finite fibre-count,
  decidability and no-section lemmas are mechanised, `--safe --without-K`, zero
  postulates. Missing: any partial-section or multi-witness layer.
  `EchoTruncation.agda` is unwired from `All.agda` and so counts as dead code
  under the repo's own discipline. Retracted and gated: the graded comonad,
  the cone universal property, any 2-categorical shape, EI-2 (gate G-001).
  Hard limit the theory itself states: any fibre-determined functional is
  witness-blind, so a confidence score may rank fibres but never choose a
  member of one.
* tropical-types: `Resource.Closure` defines the Kleene star `matStar`
  parametrically over any `ResourceAlgebra` with monotonicity, `I ⊑ A*` and a
  bound certificate, instantiated at `MinMax` (bottleneck) and `MinPlus`
  (product on quantised −log confidence). It proves **no walk semantics**;
  that exists only in Isabelle, over naturals, for max-plus and min-plus, and
  the Isabelle status is self-contradictory inside the repo. Carriers are
  ℕ-based: a quantisation layer is needed for f64 confidence.
  `hub_ceiling` is over `List TransportClass` and does not transfer literally.

=== 2.5 Estate policy (standards @ e064539, mathesis)

First-party Rust behaviour is verified with Creusot, never a parallel model.
Idris2 carries API, ABI and wire-format contracts. Theory lives in Agda as
source of truth, rechecked in Rocq and Lean. Existing Rocq proofs of theory are
target-only evidence until an Agda source exists and the statements are
compared. Status vocabulary: PROVEN, PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS, TESTED (with bounds),
ASSUMED, OPEN; a row is only as strong as its weakest dependency. Python is
banned with no exceptions, which rules out any ML regenerator the README
alludes to. squeakwell has no row in the mathesis catalogue because it has no
proof files of its own.

== 3. The invariants, restated honestly

[cols="1,2,3,2"]
|===
| Invariant (README) | Verdict | Repaired statement | Home

| MonotonicConvergence
  `drift(i+1) ≤ drift(i)`
| **False.** Runnable witness: a Document-only octad reports
  `graph_document_drift {0.0, computable:false}`; add one graph edge whose
  target is not in the text and it becomes `{0.5, computable:true}`.
| Progress on a lexicographic measure `mu(o) = (|missing o|, nconflicts o)`
  over naturals: `step o = o ∨ mu(step o) <lex mu(o)`, with `step = normalise ∘
  fill`, guarded fill (None→Some only), normalise copying the winner and never
  un-filling, hypotheses `conflict_refl`, `conflict_sym`, `winner_present`,
  `winner_stable`, and `nconflicts` counted relative to the winner. Fixpoint in
  at most 9 steps. Drift is an observable, related only by a bridge lemma
  (`nconflicts = 0 → every computable pairwise drift = 0`).
| Rocq `formal/Recovery.v` (recheck leg); Agda `EchoPartialSection` (source).

| IdentityPreservation
  `infer(e,m1) consistent_with observe(e,m2)`
| **Split.** The identity half is a three-line induction over the existing
  `*_preserves_id` lemmas. The consistency half is not a theorem: it contradicts
  the existence of Phase 3 and depends on oracles.
| `oid (iter step n o) = oid o`, with fill defined as `merge (fun a _ => a) o
  (mk_octad (oid o) (oracle o))` so the observed value wins, and all oracles
  passed as arguments, never declared as Parameters. Plus: guarded fill never
  changes an observed value on the six content modalities; the two audit
  modalities (provenance, temporal) move by chain prefix, not by equality.
| Rocq `formal/Recovery.v`.

| DeterministicResolution
  `resolve(c,I) = resolve(c,I)`
| **Vacuous** (reflexivity). The Rust is order-dependent: LWW takes the
  caller's last element; `ModalityPriority` falls back to `.first()` for
  modalities not in the list.
| A pure Rust kernel `resolve(policy, &[(Modality, Ts, Mval)]) -> Option<Modality>`
  with `#[ensures(result == spec_resolve(policy, xs@))]`, body free of clock,
  RNG and statics; `ModalityPriority` permutation-invariant given a total order
  on `Modality` as tie-break; `LastWriter` permutation-invariant only with
  timestamps carried in the input and a total tie-break; equality on
  observable fields only.
| Creusot on `src/engine/resolve.rs` (squeakwell).

| TypeRatchetSoundness
  `passes(e,n) → ∀ m ≤ n. passes(e,m)`
| **Belongs to vcl-ut**, and is Statement-indexed, not data-indexed. Holds by
  constructor shape; the projection function is unwritten. Transitions have no
  level index at all (`certifiedTransitionLevel` is the constant 4).
| `weaken : SafetyCertificate s sch n -> CanWeakenStar n m -> SafetyCertificate
  s sch m` (about 23 clauses). squeakwell's `passes(e, k)` is defined as: the
  canonical reintroduction statements for e certify at level ≥ k; capped at 6
  from text, 4 for transitions.
| Idris2 `vcl-ut/src/core/Levels.idr`; quoted contract in squeakwell's ABI.

| TerminationGuarantee
  `∃ i. drift < θ ∨ i = max_iterations`
| **Vacuous** (satisfied by `i := max_iterations`); no loop exists.
| Well-foundedness of `mu` on nat×nat gives termination without fuel;
  `max_iterations` is an operational budget and a `NotConverged` error, not the
  argument.
| Same file as Progress.

| "Never export corrupt data" (PROOF-NEEDS)
| Not statable over `project M` (normalise legitimately overwrites a drifted
  observed value, so the repaired octad leaves that fibre).
| At fragment granularity: let `T ⊆ dom(obs)` be the fragments Phase 3 accepts
  as authoritative (an output of recovery, recorded in provenance; every
  overruled fragment carries a `RepairJustification`). Every exported octad e
  satisfies `restrict_T e = obs|_T`, i.e. `e : Echo restrict_T (obs|_T)`.
  Review-queue completeness is stated over the *enumerated candidate fibre*
  (`FiberSize-fin (obs ∘ cand_y) y`), not over the mask fibre, and needs a
  concrete `mval` with decidable equality.
| Agda `EchoRecovery` audience record (source); Creusot export postcondition.
|===

== 4. Architecture

=== 4.1 The four options as judged

Scores are 1–10 from the three judges (elegance E, dependability/privacy D,
speed-to-first-recovery S). All four honour the hard requirement that
VeriSimDB is the underlying database.

[cols="2,1,1,1,4"]
|===
| Option | E | D | S | Decisive finding

| A. Library-coupled: verisim crates in-process, server at export
| 7/7/6 | 6/7/7 | 5/5/5
| Best offline testability and data minimisation; but re-implements two
  private server semantics (computability flags, hash embedding) with no
  compile-time tie, and `verisim-normalizer` pulls `verisim-octad` and all
  eight modality crates, so the "light" fallback does not exist.

| B. Server-driven: every fragment is an octad in verisim-api
| 8/7/8 | 7/5/8 | 4/4/3
| Cleanest separation and the strongest form of the requirement; but the MVP
  is blocked on six upstream route additions, has zero offline test surface,
  and writes every raw and contradictory value to an unauthenticated,
  all-interfaces server before any review.

| C. Hybrid with the session dogfooded as an octad
| 5/6/5 | 6/5/6 | 4/4/5
| The `verify` subcommand and the fibre trichotomy are good; the dogfood
  layer fills modalities with semantically void content (hash-embedding of
  review text, an 8×8 drift tensor) and leaks the review queue to the server
  every iteration.

| D. Compiler: fragments → certified VCL program → lowered to REST
| 7/8/7 | 7/7/8 | 5/6/8
| Dissolves the README's category error (the certified object *is* a
  statement), replay is a testable WAL-style property, fail-closed round abort
  on a missing gate, values never enter statement text. Weaknesses: verisimdb
  executes none of the lifecycle verbs, so the lowering is a second semantics
  guarded only by replay tests; NORMALISE lowering is intent-lossy today;
  round-0 lowers all fragments before review unless A's classification is
  grafted in front.
|===

=== 4.2 Recommendation

Per estate doctrine 15 the most elegant correct long-term option is the
default arm, and it must be labelled: **D with A's kernel in front (default
arm)**. Named here as *the compiler route*.

* From D: the program IR and canonical printer, the gate client on the frozen
  binary seam, the lowering with bindings and statement-hash provenance, the
  round-based journal with `replay` and `diff`, intent/effect reconciliation,
  round abort on `GateUnavailable`.
* From A: local-first fragment typing, the pure resolve kernel with its law
  tests, guarded fill, the lexicographic measure and the mandatory
  counter-model test, `quality: Option<f64>` that is `None` when nothing is
  computable, the constructor that refuses verisimdb's `SummaryRegenerator`,
  the id-map written before any dependent write.
* From C: the fibre trichotomy (size 0/1/n) as the export gate with confidence
  as ranking only, and the `verify` subcommand that rebuilds status from the
  id-map and the server alone.
* From B: the upstream asks (section 5.1), filed with measured numbers from
  the MVP run rather than argued.

Why not B outright, although one judge preferred it: its MVP cannot run
without verisimdb changes the squeakwell owner does not control, and it has no
offline test surface, which is exactly what an ultracode session needs to
validate within one sitting. Why not A outright: it duplicates private server
semantics and its `--allow-offline` switch reintroduces fail-open. The compiler
route keeps the server as the only drift authority and keeps squeakwell's own
code to typing, synthesis, lowering and journalling.

Alternatives rated for the record (elegance/correctness first, then
dependability):

. **D-only** (no local kernel, lower everything at round 0): most elegant on
  paper, loses on privacy and on recovery power until verisimdb grows
  `propose`/`apply`. Second.
. **A-only**: dependable offline, slower to a first recovery, duplicates
  semantics. Third.
. **B**: correct end state for the server, but blocked. Fourth today, first
  once section 5.1 lands upstream; the compiler route converges to it.

=== 4.4 The honest grading rule

For each entity e squeakwell emits one `DECLARE <mod>.<field> FROM OCTAD 'e'
WHERE <mod>.<field> = $n LIMIT 1` per populated field and, for each repair, a
`NORMALISE 'e' FROM AUTHORITATIVE <mod>`. The gate verdict on those statements
is what squeakwell reports: `gate_level 6` means the field names resolve in the
recovered schema and the statement shape passes L0–L6; `4` means the transition
is admissible. It says nothing about the values (they travel in `$n`), L2 is
near-vacuous on parsed input, and L7–L10 are reported as *not expressible from
text (vcl-ut #25)*, never as achieved. Phase 5 is re-scoped from "levels 7–10"
to: fixpoint reached, gate-admissible at 6/4, provenance chain verifies, server
drift agrees with local expectation.

=== 4.5 Aspects the design must honour regardless of option

* **Fail-closed.** Remove every `.ok()` on client calls; `recover` refuses to
  start without `GET /health`; a missing or timed-out gate aborts the round
  before any network effect; no offline switch for `recover`.
* **No silent green.** Raw `verisim-drift` calculators are never used without a
  computable flag; `quality` is `Option<f64>`; server-dependent tests *skip with
  a message*, never pass, when the server or gate is absent.
* **Integrity and provenance.** GET after every PUT (the server under-reports
  presence on update); one provenance record per executed statement keyed by
  its SHA-256; `GET /provenance/{id}/verify` before export; the server's actor
  string is client-supplied, so the chain is tamper-evident, not authenticated.
* **Privacy.** Fragments, params and the review queue stay in the workdir;
  only agreed, singleton-fibre values are lowered; bind the recovery server to
  loopback or the podman network, enable auth, build the persistent image with
  a retention policy; the FNV hash-embedding is a content fingerprint and is
  labelled non-semantic.
* **Security of the gate seam.** Spawn `vclt-gate` by absolute pinned path, no
  shell, stdin JSON only; record its commit hash in every certificate.
* **Licensing.** squeakwell, verisimdb and `vcltotal-parse` are MPL-2.0;
  echo-types carries an AGPL-3.0-or-later text in `LICENSES/` that must be
  checked before any Agda is vendored; pin every git dependency by revision.
* **Language policy.** No Python, Go or TypeScript anywhere, including
  regenerators: only deterministic regeneration ships.

== 9. Decisions for the owner

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-05 through the selector. The default arm per
doctrine 15 is marked; where the owner departed from it, the departure is
recorded with its consequence.

. **Coupling arm: the compiler route (D with A's kernel).** Default arm,
  accepted. Option B-first would have waited on six verisimdb routes; A-only
  would have duplicated two private server semantics; C added session mapping
  and a privacy leak channel for no recovery power.
. **Proof source of truth: Agda first in echo-types.** Default arm, accepted.
  `EchoPartialSection.agda` lands first; `verisimdb/formal/Recovery.v` is its
  instantiation and the statements are compared per PROOF-POLICY section 3.
  Consequence: the first mechanised result waits on an Agda toolchain being
  installed where agents run (section 10); the R0 property tests are the
  interim evidence.
. **vcl-ut extension: Option 2, a `RECOVER` verb with an evidence slot.**
  *Departure from the default arm* (additive `RepairJustification`
  constructors). The owner chose the verb. Consequence: `TRecover SubjectRef
  RepairJustification (Maybe Expr) SafetyLevel` and `Grammar.Verb VRecover`
  reopen the totality cases in `transitionEvidence`, `transitionLevel`,
  `transitionVerb`, `subjectsDistinct`, `decTransition`, `enc_transition` and
  the gate's `transition_shape`; `certifiedSubjectsDistinct` survives if
  `transitionAdmissible` keeps its conjunctive shape. The evidence slot gives
  `RECOVER e WHERE drift < $1` the L4 injection-polarity check that NORMALISE
  lacks. A rung above 4 still needs a new decider and soundness lemma in the
  `Transition.idr` reflection style (for example `residueBounded`). The
  additive `Residue` constructors of Option 1 ride inside the same change as the
  justification payload, so Option 2 strictly contains Option 1. The upstream
  issue (section 10) is filed as Option 2 with this list of reopened cases;
  until it lands, squeakwell lowers `RECOVER` lines to `NORMALISE FROM
  AUTHORITATIVE` and records the downgrade in the certificate log.
. **The L10 conjecture: parked** behind the three vcl-ut fixes it needs;
  squeakwell's epistemic output is labelled Believes (KD45) until a
  completeness argument for the enumeration exists.
. **Name: `squeakwell` is kept.** The "Octad-Recover" working title is retired;
  the README's naming table stays as the record of the alternatives considered.
. **Zig FFI: `src/interface/ffi` is deleted.** A one-paragraph README records
  that an Idris2 ABI must exist before a C-ABI bridge means anything.

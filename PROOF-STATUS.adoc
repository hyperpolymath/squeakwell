== Status vocabulary

PROVEN (kernel-checked, no axioms beyond the prover's logic) ·
PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS (kernel-checked; the axioms are listed) · TESTED (runnable
check, not a proof) · ASSUMED (used as a hypothesis, not discharged) · OPEN.
